Garden centres & greenhouses

Heat and compost from your own green waste

Your crop and pruning waste no longer has to be taken away. The system turns it into heat for the greenhouse or building in the cold months, and into high-grade compost that you use on your own land or sell on in the garden centre. A closed loop on your own site.

  • Heat for greenhouse or building when you need it
  • High-grade compost to use yourself or sell on
  • Processing of your own crop and pruning waste on your own site
  • Less dependent on gas and grid congestion

Dairy & livestock farms

Heat for the barn, better soil on the land

Livestock farms have residual streams and a heat demand. The system heats your buildings and delivers compost that improves soil structure. Unlike raw manure, livestock can return to the land sooner. You do not have to wait three weeks before the grass is grazed again.

  • Heat for barn, house or farm building
  • Process your own manure and organic material
  • Soil-improving compost for your own land
  • Livestock back on the land sooner than with raw manure

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Is your question not here? Get in touch, we are glad to think along about your situation.

How much space do I need?

You need 30 m² for one system. Systems can be placed side by side and linked, depending on your heat demand. We look at the best spot on your site together.

Which residual streams can I use?

Crop residues, green waste, verge cuttings and manure, among others. During the advisory call we look at which streams you have available and what the best mix is.

How much work is it for me?

Little. The system runs itself. The control monitors temperature and process automatically, so you do not have to check daily and nobody has to be freed up for it. Two to six times a year you take the compost out and new residual streams go in. With a loader that is half a day of work for one person, per system.

How long does the composting process take?

The process is steered precisely from a distance. That lets you set the system to a cycle of one month to six months, depending on the size of your residual streams and your demand for heat and compost.

Do I need a gas or heavy electrical connection?

No. The system delivers heat without a gas connection and without a heavy electrical connection. Four systems together run off one ordinary socket. That keeps the installation simple and means it also works at sites where grid congestion rules other systems out.

What does a system cost?

A system is a tailored investment, depending on your heat demand and residual streams. After a short advisory call we put together a fitting proposal. You can already make a trial calculation on our website under “Calculate”.
